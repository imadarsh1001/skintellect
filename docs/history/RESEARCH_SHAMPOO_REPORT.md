### Key Observations across the Formulations:
1. **Sulfate-Free Status:** Products 1, 2, 3, 4a, 4b, 5, 6, and 7 are genuine **sulfate-free** cleansers using mild alternative surfactants (e.g., Decyl Glucoside, Sodium Cocoyl Isethionate, Cocamidopropyl Betaine, Sodium Lauroyl Sarcosinate). Only **Product 8 (Sebamed Color Care)** contains *Sodium Laureth Sulfate (SLES)* and **Product 9 (Beauty Garage Botoliss Acidic Bonding)** contains *Sodium Coceth Sulfate* (an ethoxylated coconut sulfate).
2. **Silicone Profiles:** Products 1, 2, 3, 4a, 4b, and 5 contain silicones (*Dimethicone*, *Amodimethicone*, or *Dimethiconol*) to impart smoothness, frizz control, and slip. Products 6, 7, 8, and 9 are **100% Silicone-free**.
3. **Fungal Acne / Seborrheic Dermatitis Safety:** **Product 6 (New Anti-Dandruff Shampoo)** is the **ONLY 100% Malassezia / Fungal Acne Safe** shampoo in this entire lineup. All other shampoos contain fatty acids, esters (like *Glycol Distearate*, *PEG-150 Distearate*, *PEG-55 Propylene Glycol Oleate*), or plant oils that feed Malassezia yeast.
4. **Preservative & Sensitizer Alert:** **Product 2 (L'Oréal Xtenso Care)** contains *Methylchloroisothiazolinone (MCI)* and *Methylisothiazolinone (MI)*, which are strong contact allergens restricted in many leave-on formulas and strictly capped in rinse-off products. **Products 4a & 4b** contain *DMDM Hydantoin* (a formaldehyde-releaser).
